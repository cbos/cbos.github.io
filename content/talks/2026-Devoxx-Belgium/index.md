  abstract:
    Debugging a consistently failing unit test is easy; you set a breakpoint and fix it. But how do you debug a "random" failure in a massive microservices landscape or a distributed monolith?<BR>
    <BR>
    During an interview, I was once told, "You are more of a debugger than a coder." They were right. I thrive on the bugs that make others give up the nasty, intermittent issues hidden under the carpet.<BR>
    But you cannot solve these issues with 'System.out.println()' or a simple debugger attached to localhost.<BR>
    <BR>
    Debugging at scale requires a shift in mindset and tooling. It requires moving from the paradigm of "5 Whys" and 'logger.info' to a structured observational approach based on observability data.<BR>
    Based on production outages, I'll share how I uncovered the problems, the lessons I learned and how you can combine a debugging mindset with observability tooling like OpenTelemetry and the Grafana toolset to gain deeper insights.<BR>
